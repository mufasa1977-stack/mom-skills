# Digital-Product Empire Playbook — the repeatable system

> **THE ONE LAW that everything else serves:** *The $17 PDF is a TRIPWIRE, not the business.* Its only job is to convert a stranger into a **buyer** (buyers convert to the backend 5–10x better than free subscribers) and to **capture their email**. The business is: **buyer email list × a $47–$297 backend × a recurring tier.** Every operator below — Welsh, Koe, Barry — makes the front-end product cheap on purpose and makes the real money on the backend and the list. If you optimize the $17 and neglect the list + backend, you have a product, not a business.

## THE 6-PART SYSTEM

### 1. OFFER — make a $17 PDF feel like a $500 value (Hormozi)

**The Value Equation — the master formula:**

> **Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)**

The top two *create* value; the bottom two *destroy* it. Most sellers only inflate the top. The leverage is **crushing the bottom.** On a cheap digital product:
- **Dream Outcome ↑** — sell an identity/status shift, not the file. Not "a UFO PDF" → "know what the Pentagon spent 60 years hiding."
- **Perceived Likelihood ↑** — proof stack (screenshots, doc citations, a *named repeatable system*) + a guarantee. This is where low-ticket wins or dies.
- **Time Delay ↓** — engineer a *fast first win*; include a "read-this-first" quick-start.
- **Effort & Sacrifice ↓** — ship **tools/checklists/templates/swipe files**, not more reading. *"Tools and checklists beat additional trainings."* Every "you'll have to figure out X" is a value leak — pre-solve it.

**The bonus stack (this is what makes $27 feel like $500).** The 11 rules, compressed: every bonus gets (a) a **benefit-in-the-title name**, (b) a **stated dollar value**, (c) ties to a **specific fear/obstacle**, and (d) the **total bonus value must eclipse the core price.** Example stack → *stated value ≈ $452, price $27* — the delta IS the perceived deal. **Never discount the core price; add bonuses instead** (protects price integrity).

**Guarantee (reverse the risk — Hormozi's #1 lever).** At $17–50 with ~$0 fulfillment cost, **lead with a broad UNCONDITIONAL money-back** ("30-day, no questions") — few refund a cheap item and the conversion lift dwarfs refund cost. Add a light **conditional** ("do X, or your money back") only if you see refund abuse — it filters tire-kickers.

**Name it (MAGIC formula):** **M**agnet + **A**vatar + **G**oal + **I**nterval + **C**ontainer. Container words ("Field Guide," "System," "Blueprint," "Toolkit," "Vault") out-perform "PDF/ebook." A name that states *avatar + outcome + timeframe* pre-sells.

### 2. PRICING / TIERS

- **$17 is the repeated magic tripwire price** (range $7–$27). Charm pricing ($17/$27/$47) exploits left-digit bias (~8–12% lift). Pricing *too low* ($5) lowers perceived likelihood — "if it's $5 it can't work."
- On Gumroad specifically, **$30–$49 converts ~28% better than sub-$10** when positioned as premium; sub-$10 signals disposability. Choose lane deliberately: **$17 = tripwire** (job is buyer creation), **$30–$49 = premium standalone.**
- **Offer 2–3 tiers → ~2× revenue** vs single tier (PDF alone / PDF+templates+workshop / high tier).
- **Pay-What-You-Want is a LIST-BUILDING tool, not a revenue tool:** ~8% more sales but avg price collapses $66→$18. Use PWYW/$0-$1 to harvest emails; use fixed price to make money.

### 3. FUNNEL — the exact order

```
Free lead magnet  →  $17 tripwire  →  order bump (+~40%)  →  upsell/OTO (+~20%)  →  $47–$297 core/bundle  →  recurring tier
   (email course)      (creates a        (checkbox at          (one-time offer          (to the BUYER list)     (community/
                        BUYER + email)    checkout)             post-purchase)                                    membership)
```

**Attach the two highest-ROI add-ons to EVERY checkout — this is where the money is, not the front PDF:**
- **Order bump** (checkbox add-on at checkout): **20–60% take, avg ~40%.** A $9–$27 checklist/templates/swipe file.
- **First upsell / OTO** (one-click post-purchase): **~20% take** at $47–$97.

Worked math on a $17 PDF at 588 sales/mo: order bump ~40%×$15 ≈ **+$3,500/mo**; upsell ~20%×$67 ≈ **+$7,900/mo** — the add-ons dwarf the $10k of front-end.

### 4. EMAIL LIST — "the list is the asset" (owned vs rented)

Social is **rented land** (discovery); the email list is **owned land** (where the buy happens). Email ROI is famously ~**$38 per $1**; rule of thumb ~**$1/subscriber/month** if actively monetized.

- **Capture:** put a **free lead-magnet opt-in in FRONT of the paid product** to catch the 90%+ who don't buy today. Lead-magnet opt-in benchmarks: 3–8.6% site-wide, **20–40%+ on a dedicated landing page**, popup-with-image 5.46% > 3.22% without.
- **Tag `buyer` vs `freebie` separately** — buyers are your highest-LTV segment for the next product.
- **Nurture sequence that sells (Dan Koe's pattern):** Email 1 (welcome) delivers the magnet + pitches the tripwire, with a **PS seeding a cheap recurring offer at a $5-first-month hook** → Email 2 *mentions* the recurring offer (no link, builds desire) → Email 3 *links* it → Email 4 pure value, **no pitch** (goodwill). Then: **one single-topic value email per week, each ending in ONE CTA to the product that matches that topic.**

### 5. TRAFFIC — one piece of content, many channels, all → email

**Koe's "one piece of content" loop (the engine):** write ONE weekly anchor (newsletter/video, ~45 min) → rip to YouTube → podcast → 6–8 short-form clips (X threads, IG carousels, LinkedIn, Shorts/Reels/TikTok). One idea, ~7 channels, **every CTA points to the email opt-in.** Welsh staggers this over 4–6 weeks so the newsletter is always being promoted.

**Short-form → product CTA structure that actually converts (don't bet only on "link in bio"):**
- Layer the CTA: behavioral cue at **sec 10–20**, explicit product moment **sec 20–35**, closing instruction at the end.
- **Comment-keyword CTA** ("comment UFO and I'll DM the link") gets **1.5–2.2× more comments** AND delivers the link by DM, dodging link-in-bio friction (documented conversion killer). "Save this" lifts Reels reach 7–14 days.
- Watchers convert **30–40% more** than non-watchers; deliver a complete micro-win, THEN point to "the full guide."
- **Content-to-pitch ratio ~80/20** (4 value : 1 pitch) — over-pitching kills reach.
- Volume is the mechanic: faceless short-form works on *impressions × tiny conversion*, not per-video virality. Keep **5–8 links in bio**, not 1, not 20.

### 6. BACKEND / RECURRING — where the real money lives

The front PDF is the entry point; **recurring + high-ticket is the business.** Progression that every operator ran: cheap recurring community/membership ($29/mo, seeded at $5 first month) → high-ticket transformation tier ($499→$999→$8,000, with a "$500 difference" upgrade anchor) → eventually a **productized backend** (cohort, membership, or software/fund). Welsh's memberships hit $19k MRR; Koe's $8k PhD tier is the margin driver; Barry's endgame was SaaS.

## EXECUTABLE CHECKLIST (do these, in order)

1. **Reframe the product name** with MAGIC (avatar + outcome + timeframe + a Container word like "Field Guide/Vault").
2. **Build the value stack** on the sales page: core value $X, then 4–6 bonuses each named-with-benefit, priced, and tied to one fear — total stated value must eclipse price ≥10×.
3. **Add a broad unconditional 30-day money-back guarantee** (reverse the risk).
4. **Price the front at $17** (tripwire) OR $30–$49 (premium standalone) — pick the lane on purpose. Add 2–3 tiers.
5. **Wire an order bump (~40% take) + one post-purchase upsell (~20% take)** at checkout — this is the profit, not the front PDF.
6. **Put a FREE lead magnet in front** of the paid product to capture the 90% who don't buy today; pipe emails to an ESP.
7. **Tag `buyer` vs `freebie`;** build the 4-email nurture (pitch → seed recurring → link recurring → pure value).
8. **Ship one anchor content piece/week → 6–8 short-form clips → every CTA to the email opt-in.** Use comment-keyword CTAs on short-form.
9. **Send one single-topic value email/week**, each ending in ONE CTA to the matching product.
10. **Stand up a recurring tier** ($9–$29/mo community, seeded at $5 first month) to the buyer list.
11. **Build ONE backend offer above the tripwire** ($47–$297) — the buyer list × this offer is where $10k/mo comes from.
12. **Reprice the core 3–4x once product/audience fit is proven** (Welsh's single biggest lever) — don't leave money on the table.

## THE VOLUME MATH (so you know what "working" looks like)

`Revenue = Traffic × Opt-in% × Conversion% × Price × (1 + backend multiplier)`

- $10k/mo at $17 = **~588 sales/mo ≈ 20/day.** At $19, 526/mo ≈ 18/day.
- Tripwire conversion: **cold 2–5%, warm 10–15%.** Order bump ~40%. Upsell ~20%.
- Realistic path: ~50k–100k short-form views/day → comment-keyword/bio → free magnet (25–30% opt-in) → 2–5% buy the $17 → the **growing buyer list × a $97–$297 backend at 2% = ~$9,700 on one promo.** The list compounds; the $17 alone never gets you there.

**Caveat:** these are industry-cited benchmark *ranges*, not guarantees — actual rates swing by niche, traffic temperature, and offer quality. Treat them as targets to beat, and optimize the ONE funnel stage furthest below benchmark, not all at once.
